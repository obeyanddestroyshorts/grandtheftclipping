Privacy Policy: Grand Theft Clipping Stats

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Grand Theft Clipping Stats ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Grand Theft Clipping YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

  • Statistics of the owner's own YouTube channel and videos (views, likes, comments, average view duration, subscribers gained), read through the YouTube Data API v3 and the YouTube Analytics API.
  • Permission to upload videos to the owner's own channel.

The app does not access other users' data, contacts, email, or any other Google service.

How the data is used and stored

The statistics are stored only on the owner's private computer and shown in a private dashboard on the owner's home network. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored statistics can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen, or message @grandtheftclipping.
